# My Favorite Series

Here is a collection of my top three favorite animated series, each known for incredible storytelling, complex characters, and intense action.

---

## 1. Arcane
**Arcane** is an animated steampunk action-adventure series set in the *League of Legends* universe. It explores the growing tensions between the utopian city of **Piltover** and the oppressed underground city of **Zaun**.
* **Key Characters:** Jinx, Vi, Jayce, Viktor, Caitlyn
* **Why I Love It:** Top not character development, story and plot are superb as always, all characters have their own history and problems which makes it really down to earth and relatable at times.

> *"In the pursuit of great, we failed to do good."* — Viktor

---

## 2. Vinland Saga
**Vinland Saga** is a historical epic anime following **Thorfinn**, a young boy raised by Vikings who seeks revenge against the mercenary leader Askeladd, before eventually embarking on a profound journey toward peace and redemption.
* **Key Characters:** Thorfinn, Askeladd, Canute, Thors
* **Why I Love It:** Incredible character development, gripping philosophical themes about violence and true strength, and realistic historical grit.

> *"I have no enemies."* — Thors

---

## 3. Saga of Tanya the Evil (*Youjo Senki*)
**Saga of Tanya the Evil** is a dark fantasy military *isekai* series. After being reborn into an alternate, magic-infused version of World War I Europe, a ruthless salaryman becomes **Tanya Degurechaff**, a young aerial mage climbing the Imperial military ranks to defy a mysterious entity known as Being X.
* **Key Characters:** Tanya Degurechaff, Viktoriya Ivanovna Serebryakov, Erich von Rerugen
* **Why I Love It:** Consists of military mechanics with aerial and magic mixed into it which makes it really interesting and unique.
